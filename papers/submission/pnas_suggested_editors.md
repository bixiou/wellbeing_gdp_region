# PNAS — suggested editors

For Direct Submissions, authors may suggest editors among the PNAS Member Editors (NAS members) and Editorial Board members. Each person below is listed in the NAS **PNAS Member Editor directory** ([search page](https://nrc88.nas.edu/pnas_search/default.aspx)), checked on 1 October 2026. The institution and fields come from their directory entry. The PNAS editorial-board page (pnas.org/about/editorial-board) blocked automated access, so current board membership could not be checked there.

**Suggested choice:** Deaton (economics of well-being), Groves (survey methodology) and King or Gelfand (cross-group comparability, culture).

**Note on Deaton:** he is the best-qualified editor on the substance. The paper discusses and partly qualifies his 2008 results (the WVS sampling hypothesis, and the growth effect, which it replicates and then explains). That is not a conflict of interest, but you may prefer to list him as a reviewer or editor knowing he may have a view on it.

Check for conflicts (coauthors in the last 48 months, same institution) before submitting.
